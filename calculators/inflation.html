Inflation Calculator

See what today's money will cost — and be worth — in the future.

How this calculator works

Enter an amount, an inflation rate and a number of years. The calculator shows what the same basket of goods would cost in the future and the purchasing power of today's money at that point.

Why it matters

Inflation quietly reduces the value of cash. Savings earning less than inflation lose purchasing power every year. Use this tool to set future goals in realistic numbers — for education, retirement income or any long-term target.

Frequently asked questions

What inflation rate should I use?

Use your central bank's target or recent averages for your country, and test a higher rate for safety.

How is this calculated?

Future cost = amount × (1 + inflation)^years.

What beats inflation?

Historically, diversified growth assets have outpaced inflation over long periods, with short-term volatility.
