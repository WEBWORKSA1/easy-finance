FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Easy.Finance really free?

Yes. Every calculator, guide and video is free. We're supported by advertising, optional reader donations, sponsorships and — only if you ask for help — referral fees from partners. See how we make money.

Is my data saved when I use the calculators?

No. Calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us.

Do you give personalised financial advice?

No. We publish education and tools. If you want personalised help, our free request form connects you with relevant professionals or services; you choose whether to proceed.

Which countries is this for?

Our tools work in multiple currencies (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, AUD, INR, LKR) and our guides explain principles that apply everywhere, with country notes where rules differ.
